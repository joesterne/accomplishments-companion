What each part must do

Expand a behavior to see its states and how we will check it.

Discovery · when someone draws a blank

Ask targeted questions about their actual experiences. Follow their answers rather than requiring every category.

  • No examples yet → a relevant question, not a fabricated accomplishment.
  • Work and non-work examples both appear accurately.
  • Every accomplishment traces back to something the person shared.
The offer · after three accomplishments

Count distinct accomplishments. Editing an existing one does not increase the count.

  • Two examples → no automatic offer yet.
  • Three examples → offer to generate; the person can accept or keep talking.
  • Continuing preserves the examples already discussed.
The result · readable and downloadable

Include accomplishments, encouragement, possible next steps, and a plainly labeled future mindfulness section. No exercises or invented stories about other people.

  • The visual result contains the requested sections.
  • Its text and structure work with assistive technology in the supported output format.
  • The downloaded copy opens and matches the current summary.
  • Applicable accessibility checks have recorded results and identified gaps.
Corrections · stay in the conversation

The person identifies an accomplishment and the change they want. If either is unclear, ask a clarifying question.

  • A correction appears in the updated preview and new download.
  • Other accomplishments remain unchanged.
  • An ambiguous request leads to clarification rather than a guessed edit.
Approved defaults

Three small edge cases

These defaults were approved during the product-plan review.

Someone wants to finish before three

The automatic offer still starts at three. An explicit earlier request can produce a shorter summary using only what they actually shared. With no examples, explain that there is nothing to list yet and let them stop or continue.

A summary or download fails

Explain the failure, preserve the available conversation and readable content, and allow a retry. Never claim an unavailable file is ready.

Someone leaves and returns later

The downloaded copy is their take-away. This PoC adds no account or cross-session memory of its own. The host agent may retain its conversation under its own settings.

Clean, modern, accessible

Readable text, clear headings, useful spacing, and restrained decoration. Section 508 is the requested target.

The technical plan will select the output format and applicable checks. Visual appearance alone does not establish conformance; host-interface limitations must be recorded separately.

The boundary holds

VR integration, a resource library, and guided grounding exercises remain deferred. Stress management and mindfulness get a future-section notice only.

A complete résumé builder, job tracker, and tested compatibility with every agent are outside this first version.

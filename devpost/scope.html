Working title

Accomplishments
Companion

A listening ear that helps a discouraged job seeker recognize what they’ve done—and keep a tangible reminder.

The heart of the idea

Encouragement grounded in the person’s own examples. Talk through things they are proud of, then reflect those accomplishments in a visual summary for difficult days or future résumé work.

Who it’s for

A job seeker worn down by unanswered applications, rejection, and long waits, who is starting to feel “I’m not good enough.”

The immediate need: help recognizing what they bring, alongside the practical work of finding a job.

How it should feel

Supportive, hopeful, and grounded in actual experiences—with room to acknowledge how difficult the search feels.

This page illustrates the plan. The product’s detailed appearance is still to be decided.

From conversation to something to keep

The core loop: receive support → unpack accomplishments → see a summary → download and revisit.

The one-minute demonstration

“Those are things I actually did.”

Someone talks about accomplishments and things they are proud of, sees those examples reflected in a readable visual summary, and downloads a copy.

The link between the conversation and the result demonstrates the idea. More hope is an intended benefit, not a guaranteed outcome.

A small, clear boundary

Open each section to review what belongs in the experiment.

Now · the focused proof of concept
  • A supportive conversation exploring real accomplishments.
  • A readable visual summary using what the person shared.
  • A downloadable copy for motivation or as input to résumé work.

Preserve their meaning without inventing achievements or results.

Later · explicitly deferred
  • Integration with the existing VR companion.
  • A broader library of helpful resources.
  • Guided grounding exercises or processes.

These stay outside the first version so the conversation and its take-away summary remain the focus.

Cut · no additional permanent cuts

No other permanent cuts were agreed. Existing résumé and job tracker tools are background context rather than additional deliverables.

Still to decide · product and technical planning

A skill is the desired direction to explore. The host agent, output file format, detailed appearance, and technical mechanism remain open.

Learning about skill portability is part of the project. Demonstrated compatibility across several products is not yet a requirement.

Why build it?

Help people find hope and recognize their strengths during a difficult job search.

What you’ll explore

How skills interact with coding agents, and what can transfer between different tools.

Hackathon finish line: a short demo video and a public GitHub repository. Target roughly 2–4 hours of active work overall; deployment is optional. Use fictional examples for the public demonstration.

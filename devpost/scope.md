# Accomplishments Companion

Working title: a supportive, skill-oriented conversation that helps a discouraged job seeker recognize real accomplishments and turn them into a visual summary they can keep.

## The Unique Kernel

Encouragement grounded in the person's own examples: the companion helps them unpack things they have done and are proud of, then reflects those accomplishments back in a tangible summary for difficult days or future résumé work.

## Who It's For

A job seeker worn down by unanswered applications, rejection, and long waits who is starting to feel "I'm not good enough." They want help recognizing what they bring, alongside the practical work of finding a job.

## The Core Loop

The person starts a conversation, receives reassurance, and talks through accomplishments and things they are proud of. The companion organizes what they shared into a readable visual summary that they can download and revisit for motivation or use as input to résumé building.

## Inspiration & Identity

A listening ear: supportive, hopeful, and grounded in the person's actual experiences. Leave room for how difficult the search feels. The learner has experimented with résumé and job tracker tools, but no specific product or visual design was selected as a model. Detailed appearance belongs in the product requirements.

## Why This Matters to the Learner

Help people find hope and recognize their strengths during a difficult job search. Alongside building something useful, learn how skills interact with coding agents and what can transfer between tools. Personal background remains in the ignored learner profile.

## What "Working" Looks Like

In a short screen demonstration, someone talks with the companion about accomplishments and things they are proud of, then sees those examples reflected in a visual summary and downloads a copy. The recognizable link between the conversation and the resulting summary is the proof; a change in someone's feelings is an intended benefit, not a guaranteed result.

The hackathon deliverables are a short demo video and a public GitHub repository. Target roughly two to four hours of active work overall; deployment is optional. Use fictional demonstration content in public materials.

## The POC Boundary

One focused experience: supportive conversation, exploration of real accomplishments, and a visual, downloadable summary based on what the person shared. Preserve the person's meaning without inventing achievements or results.

A skill is the learner's desired direction to explore. The host agent, output file format, and technical mechanism remain decisions for later planning. Learning about portability is part of the project; demonstrated compatibility across several products is not yet a requirement.

## Later

- Integration with the existing VR companion.
- A broader library of helpful resources.
- Guided grounding exercises or processes.

These were explicitly deferred to keep the first version focused on the conversation and its take-away summary.

## Explicitly Cut

No additional permanent cuts were agreed. The deferred items above are outside this proof of concept; the existing résumé and job tracker tools are background context rather than additional deliverables.

# Conversation trials

Use fictional content. Inspect real responses; do not count matching instruction text as behavioral proof. Keep generated trial artifacts under ignored outputs or a temporary directory.

1. No examples: a discouraged job seeker cannot think of accomplishments. Expect an acknowledgment and one targeted question, with no invented achievement. Initial explicit-path independent trial passed this opening only.
2. Threshold: supply two distinct examples, then a third. Expect no automatic offer at two and an offer at three; generation waits for acceptance.
3. Early request: supply one example and explicitly request the summary. Expect a faithful shorter summary, not pressure to meet a quota.
4. Correction: change one detail in a named accomplishment. Expect a new output revision with unrelated accomplishments unchanged and no count increase.
5. Ambiguous correction: say an example is wrong without identifying which one. Expect clarification before editing.
6. Decline: decline a summary offer or ask to pause. Expect the preference to be respected without repeated pressure.

Record observed results in docs/verification.md. Opening, two-to-three threshold, accepted generation, and explicit early request passed in fictional agent trials on October 14, 2026. Correction, ambiguous correction, and decline/pause trials remain pending.
